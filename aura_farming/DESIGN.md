## Brand & Style

This design system channels unapologetic neo-brutalist gaming chaos and hyper-online meme kinetic energy. Designed for competitive Gen Z mobile users hunting clout, tracking social Ws and Ls, and gaming their viral social credit, the aesthetic rejects traditional clean corporate polish. Instead, it weaponizes high-voltage video game HUD mechanics, razor-sharp geometric framing, and saturated cyber gradients against an inky obsidian void.

The visual direction combines high-contrast brutalism—raw structure, zero-radius hard borders, unpadded mechanical badges—with sensory-rich arcade tactile feedback. The emotional goal is immediate dopamine: intense visual feedback, aggressive status hierarchy, loud leaderboards, and twitch-reflex UI responsiveness. It feels like an unholy union of a high-stakes RPG stat screen, competitive fighting game damage popups, and algorithmic viral feed machinery.

## Colors

The color system operates on an electric dark-mode architecture engineered for pure screen luminance and stark contrast:

- **Primary (`#EC4899` - Hot Magenta):** The defining core accent. Used for high-stakes aura gains, critical button triggers, viral alerts, and maximum-hype callouts.
- **Secondary (`#8B5CF6` - Electric Purple):** Represents rank status, mystery tiers, and atmospheric glow states. Couples with magenta to build signature high-octane linear gradients.
- **Tertiary (`#00FF87` - Acid Neon Green):** Represents positive aura points (+10,000 AURA), live streak counters, XP replenishment, and win-state validation.
- **Accent Blue (`#06B6D4` - Cyber Cyan):** Utilized strictly for secondary data metrics, shield/buff bars, and utility navigation.
- **Neutral Base (`#0A0A0F` - Deep Obsidian):** The foundational ground. Darker than standard charcoal to ensure neon hues visually ignite off the screen without muddying contrast.
- **Surface Elevation (`#161622` to `#222238`):** Deep plum-tinted dark layers for containers and cards, maintaining a solid, ink-heavy canvas.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is intentionally jarring, combining the sculptural, ultra-wide authority of **Syne** with the off-kilter, micro-quirky velocity of **Bricolage Grotesque**.

- **Display & Headings (Syne):** Reserved for aggressive score readouts, rank unlocks, and meme declarations. Headings MUST be styled uppercase with compressed tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) and compact line heights to hit with the physical punch of a video game splash title.
- **Body & Mechanics (Bricolage Grotesque):** Used for stat descriptions, lore text, and social feeds. It maintains razor legibility under low light while preserving an idiosyncratic internet edge.
- **Labels & Tactical HUD Badges:** Always set in heavy weight Bricolage Grotesque (`700` or `800`), transformed to strict uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to mimic retro fighting game parameter stamps and system warnings.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an airtight, dense 4-column grid on mobile (expanding to 8-column on tablet and 12-column on desktop/web overlays). Visual real estate is treated like high-intensity screen real estate in competitive titles: tight margins, minimum dead air, and immediate visibility of core counters.

- **Rhythm & Compaction:** Spacing tokens adhere strictly to a dense 4px base multiplier. Structural content stacks tightly with `space-sm` and `space-md` gaps to convey urgency.
- **Canvas Framing:** Margins are fixed at `1rem` on handheld screens to push functional action directly to the bezel edges.
- **Reflow Architecture:** On wider viewports, the application locks into a centralized max-width mobile-frame shell (480px width) flanked by floating brutalist telemetry sidecars, preserving authentic mobile ergonomics across all platforms.

## Elevation & Depth

Forget diffuse, realistic dropshadows. Depth in this design system is engineered through raw mechanical offsets and hard neon luminescence:

- **Hard Neo-Brutalist Offsets:** Surfaces elevate by shifting physically 2px to 4px along the X/Y axis above a solid `#000000` base, bordered with crisp `2px` or `3px` solid stroke outlines.
- **Hard Glow Aura Emitters:** Interactive components and high-tier stat displays replace soft shadows with vibrant, non-diffuse double-stroke outlines or directional neon edge-bleeds (e.g., `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #EC4899`).
- **Glass-Chassis Plates:** Surfaces layered above video feeds or kinetic particle visualizers leverage ultra-dark, high-opacity frosted obsidian glass (`background: rgba(10, 10, 15, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), encased in high-contrast neon-gradient borders (`#EC4899` to `#8B5CF6`).

## Shapes

The geometric rule is absolute: **Zero radius (`0px`) on all boundaries**.

No rounded pill buttons. No soft friendly corners. Every container, avatar frame, interactive trigger, stat card, and XP meter utilizes absolute 90-degree angular cuts. Visual dynamic flair is achieved exclusively through asymmetrical 45-degree corner bevels (clip-path chamfers) on prominent hero cards and primary action buttons, amplifying the aggressive, cyber-arcade UI chassis.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary ("AURA BOOST"):** Pure `#EC4899` hot magenta fill, 3px solid `#000000` border, `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #000000`. Text in heavy uppercase Syne. Hover/focus shifts the offset by +2px; active press snaps the button flush with `0px` offset, creating immediate physical feedback.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent fill, `#00FF87` acid-green 2px outline, high-contrast monospace text. On active state, shifts into inverted green-on-black colorway.

### XP & Aura Point Meters
- Segmented mechanical progress bars. No smooth animations—meters fill in discrete chunky blocks with 2px hard divider lines. Filled chunks glow with a linear gradient from `#8B5CF6` to `#EC4899`, accompanied by floating `+AURA` kinetic damage-style text popups.

### Cards & Container Chassis
- Obsidian surface (`#161622`) framed by a mandatory 2px solid border (`#8B5CF6` or `#222238`). Upper card header displays a raw terminal bracket (`[ SYSTEM // XP ]`) in label-md typography. Includes hard neon bottom-right shadow drop.

### Chips, Tags & Badges
- Unlike standard systems, tags are completely unrounded blocks. High-tier status chips use saturated magenta backgrounds with stark black typography; baseline category chips use black backgrounds with neon border accents.

### Inputs & Chat Entry
- Monospaced, zero-radius fields enclosed in 2px `#222238` frames. On focus, the border turns instant electric `#00FF87` acid green accompanied by a hard `2px 2px 0px #00FF87` underline offset. Prompt cursors are thick flashing neon blocks.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square-only checkbox primitives (`0px` corner radius) with solid 2px borders. Active state features a sharp, centered neon check or an inner inverted square block with zero easing transition.
